1. Overview & Core Privacy Promise

At PixScanX, your privacy is our highest priority. PixScanX is designed from the ground up as an offline-first image and PDF processing studio.

Our Commitment:

  • Zero Server Uploads: All document scanning, image compression, format conversions, cropping, splitting, rotating, and PDF creation are processed 100% locally on your device.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, phone number, or email) to use the app.
  • Zero Cloud Storage: Your photos, scanned IDs, documents, and created PDFs never leave your smartphone or tablet.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect, transmit, store, sell, or share:

  • Any photos, images, or documents you select or capture.
  • Any biometric or facial recognition data from passport or ID photos.
  • Any contact lists, messages, browsing history, or personal files.
  • Any precise GPS location or physical location information.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

A. Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)
Purpose: Used solely when you use the in-app document scanner or capture photos for ID/passport generation.
How It Works: Camera frames are streamed directly to your local screen in memory. No pictures or video feeds are ever sent over the network or viewed by anyone else.
Optional: You can deny camera access and still use all file compression, conversion, and editing tools by picking existing images from your gallery.

B. Device Storage / Photo Picker
(READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Scoped Storage)
Purpose:

  • To allow you to pick images or PDF documents from your device for local editing, compression, or conversion.
  • To save your finished exported PDFs and images into your local device folders (such as the standard Downloads and Pictures directories).

Security: PixScanX uses modern Android Photo Picker and Scoped Storage APIs to only access files that you explicitly choose.

C. Internet & Network State
(android.permission.INTERNET, android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)
Purpose: Used exclusively to display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.
Important: PixScanX's internal image and document processing algorithms do not use the internet. You can even use all core features in Airplane Mode.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep PixScanX free for everyone, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Play Services Ads).

Information Collected by Google AdMob:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device make, model, operating system version, and general network connection type
  • Ad interactions and view performance

Google's use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Partner Technologies Policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

Users can opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android device settings:
Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. Data Security

  • Files are saved in secure standard Android storage.
  • No remote databases exist where your files could be breached or intercepted.
  • Temporary preview caches are cleared automatically when you finish or discard an operation.
